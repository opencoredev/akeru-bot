Desktop setup now asks what you want your bot to help with and shows a short plan based on your answer. The last step drafts your first message from that plan. After you send it, setup waits until your bot has started, plays a short one-time celebration, and opens the chat with your message already there. With reduced motion turned on, the plan appears right away and the celebration is skipped.
