Delete a bot from its settings page on web, or from chat settings on mobile. Deleting removes the bot from the roster, detaches its chats, stops its work, channels, and voice call, cancels work it sent or received, and removes its routines and skill assignments. Group chats keep their group and fall back to the boss when the deleted bot was answering. A group boss cannot be deleted until another bot takes over, and a bot cannot be deleted when its removal would leave a group with fewer than two active bots.
