Redesign the mobile app around the bot roster. Home becomes a messenger-style bot list: flat bot avatars with presence dots, the bot's name, the chat title as a task chip, a timestamp, and a status preview line, under a minimal header with a profile menu, search, and new chat. Chat screens lead with the bot: the header shows the bot's name and avatar, and the composer asks the bot by name. User messages render in neutral gray bubbles instead of blue.
