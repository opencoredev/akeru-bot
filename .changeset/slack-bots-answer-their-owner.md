Slack and Discord bots now answer only you and the people you add. Link your account by sending the bot a one-time code from Settings > Channels, and add extra user IDs per bot. After you mention a bot in a thread, it answers your later replies there without another mention, and stops when you reply "stop", after 24 quiet hours, or when you mention a different bot. Other people's mentions cost nothing, and people's names replace raw member IDs in what the bot reads.
